11 / FAQ

Questions worth
answering.

How does Zylora help my business bring in real customers?+

Zylora connects website creation, publishing, lead capture, appointments, approved business knowledge and optional AI-assisted visitor conversations in one workspace.

Can I start with a blank Studio?+

Open a blank Studio, shape the page directly and continue with the existing visual editing and publishing workflow.

Can I use my own custom domain?+

Connect a custom domain through the workspace domain and publishing workflow; availability and SSL status depend on the configured domain.

Does the assistant answer from information I approve?+

The assistant is configured to use approved business information and supported website context for visitor questions and next steps.
