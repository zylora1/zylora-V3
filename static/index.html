Questions

What should I know before I start?

Does AI creation require a starting template?

No. AI creation derives its page structure and design direction from the business brief. The internal rendering system is not exposed as a selectable catalogue template.

How does regional pricing work?

Starter is ₹799/month in India or US$9/month internationally, while Growth is ₹1,799/month in India or US$19/month internationally. These are fixed regional prices, not exchange-rate conversions.

Does my plan decide how many pages AI creates?

No. The AI-created page count follows the explicit brief and inferred business needs. Plans still control other account entitlements such as credits and draft capacity.

Can I edit the generated website?

Yes. The editor supports structured manual edits and AI-assisted changes before publishing.
