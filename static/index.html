Knowledge Base

Questions before you build?

Everything you need to know about Zylora's AI generation, visual studio, universal importer, and custom domain publishing.

What is Zylora? +

Zylora is an AI-first website creator and business workspace. Describe the website you need in plain English, and Zylora generates a validated SiteDocument with responsive sections, tailored copy, and built-in lead capture. You can refine everything in our Canva-grade Visual Studio.

Do I need design or coding experience? +

No. You can start with a simple sentence describing your business. Zylora's engine derives the entire site structure, typography, and content. If you want hands-on control, the Visual Studio lets you click and edit any element directly.

How does the Universal Website Importer work? +

You can upload a single .html file or a complete ZIP archive. Zylora detects frameworks including HTML/CSS/JS, React, Next.js, Angular, Vue, Nuxt, Svelte, SvelteKit, and Astro. Local assets are sanitized and copied to managed media, letting you edit existing sites visually.

Can I connect my own custom domain? +

Yes. You can attach any custom domain to your website. Zylora integrates with Cloudflare to automatically provision SSL certificates, handle HTTPS redirection, and deliver worldwide edge caching.

How does the 24/7 AI Sales Assistant work? +

The assistant is a grounded conversational chatbot widget trained specifically on your business information. It answers visitor questions, qualifies prospective leads, and directly schedules appointments into your calendar.
