Common Questions

The details, clearly.

Do I need a starting template?

No. Zylora can create your site from a natural-language business brief. Templates are also available whenever you prefer starting from an art-directed layout.

Can visitors book appointments?

Yes. The booking flow uses your configured availability rules and keeps appointments connected to your leads and analytics.

Can I edit the generated website?

Yes. Zylora Studio lets you update text, imagery, colors and supported page structures with instant preview.

How does regional pricing work?

Prices are determined automatically based on your billing country, providing fair regional rates for every market.
