Privacy Policy

This policy describes the categories of data Zylora processes to provide accounts, websites, billing, lead capture, appointments, AI features, analytics and support.

Account and business data

We process account details, Business Profile information, website content, settings, subscription records, support interactions and configuration required to operate your Zylora workspace.

Website visitor data

When a visitor uses a Zylora-hosted website, the site may process enquiry details, appointment information, AI Sales Assistant conversations, attribution such as page/referrer/UTM values, and privacy-minimized first-party analytics. Public forms include data-use notice where contact information is requested.

AI Sales Assistant

Assistant conversations are associated with the relevant website and tenant. Zylora uses business-provided profile/site/approved knowledge to answer visitor questions, and may process conversation content through the configured AI provider. Test-mode owner conversations are separated from production lead and appointment actions. Usage, retention, rate limits and deletion follow configured Zylora controls.

Communications and consent

Transactional communication needed to answer an enquiry or confirm an appointment is distinct from marketing consent. Zylora does not treat a normal service enquiry as automatic consent to receive promotional marketing.

Payments

Payment providers process payment information under their own services. Zylora stores identifiers and subscription/billing metadata needed for entitlement, reconciliation, cancellation and auditing; Zylora does not require full card data in its application database.

Security and isolation

Zylora applies tenant/site authorization controls, rate limits, signed webhook verification where configured, server-authoritative billing calculations, and safeguards intended to prevent one tenant from accessing another tenant’s business, lead, appointment or Assistant data.

Retention and deletion

Operational data is retained according to Zylora’s configured retention policies and applicable service needs. Account/site deletion workflows remove or anonymize associated data consistently with product architecture and legal/operational obligations. Assistant conversation retention is configurable rather than indefinite by default.

Third-party providers

Zylora may rely on configured infrastructure and service providers for AI, payments, email, WhatsApp, domains/security and related operations. Provider use is limited to the functionality being delivered.

Your choices

Account and site controls allow you to edit core business information and Assistant settings. Where account export or deletion is available, related Assistant/lead data is included or removed as technically and legally appropriate.

Contact

Use Zylora Support for privacy or account questions.
