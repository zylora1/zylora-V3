What is a bounty?

A bounty can be anything from creating a logo to developing an app. We just like to use it as a shorter way of saying “paid, creative task-based project”.

Find your next creative project on Bounties with upfront clarity, fees directly when you’re paid and work with secure contracts. It’s completely commission-free and open internationally.

Feeling curious and want the full intel on how Bounties and the technology behind it works? You’ve come to the right spot.

What is a bounty?⌄

A clearly scoped, paid creative project with the essentials agreed before work begins.

Who is Bounties for?⌄

Independent creatives and teams looking for thoughtful, transparent collaborations.

What’s the difference between Bounties and a traditional job board?⌄

Bounties focuses on specific outcomes, clear ownership and secure payment instead of open-ended roles.

How can I get involved?⌄

Make a profile, browse the live bounties and apply to the projects that fit your practice.

Are there any restrictions on who can sign up for Bounties?⌄

Bounties is built for an international creative community; project-specific requirements are always shown upfront.

What are the requirements for applying for a bounty?⌄

A complete profile and the portfolio material requested in the project brief.

Does Bounties take any commission on earnings?⌄

Any fees are shown clearly before you apply, so there are no surprises later.

What is a smart contract?⌄

A digital agreement that records the project terms and helps make payment transparent and secure.
