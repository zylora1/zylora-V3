# Design / IP Notes

This file is informational and is not legal advice.

Supplied design references were used as product-quality and art-direction benchmarks. The shipped product does not bundle competitor logos, screenshots, template artwork, marketing copy or font binaries from those references.

Zylora uses a text-only `Zylora` wordmark with an original product treatment. The current product typography is Inter for interface/body text and Space Grotesk for display typography and the wordmark.

The legacy template catalogue and its reference-derived/static stock assets were removed from this release. New catalogue templates can be added later only as separately reviewed designs.

A qualified trademark/IP lawyer should review the final commercial deployment and any future template catalogue before launch.
