# Zylora 21 — Content Parity & Completion Refinement

Focused refinement after review of copy density, crop ambiguity, Laurel Studio completeness, and repeated copy patterns.

## What changed

- **Review export fixed:** full-page screenshots now force off-screen `[data-reveal]` elements into their completed visible state before capture. Several templates only looked sparse because scroll-reveal content was exported at `opacity: 0`.
- **Shared content contract:** every template now maps the same six client-content needs: hero, proof, offer, work/process, about, and CTA. Layouts can express those slots differently.
- **Copy budgets:** templates are classified as compact, balanced, or editorial so punchy references stay punchy without requiring the same amount of copy as editorial references.
- **Repeated marketing copy removed:** the “Bring the … version” pattern is gone; no repeated 5+ word marketing phrase remains across distinct templates in the focused scan.
- **#17–#20 endpoint verification:** each page continues through its colored CTA block to a real footer. The review pack now includes a dedicated endpoint sheet.
- **#21 Laurel Studio completed:** the thin placeholder-like thought area was replaced with a three-project recognition/case module with distinct visual cards.
